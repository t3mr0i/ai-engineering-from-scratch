Introduction

As artificial intelligence moves from experimentation into everyday delivery, we need more than isolated experts or general a wareness
sessions. We need a shared literacy model. In this context, AI Literacy is the capability to interpret AI developments, evaluate the
relevance of AI for certain task, use AI tools appropriately, and shape outcomes with AI within the scope of one’s role and d ecision rights.

The first central idea of our model is the usage of AI capabilities. We intentionally defined the target picture for LHIND using AI capabilities
instead of specific tools or skills. Why? Tools come and go. The capabilities that we defined shall remain more stable and will serve as a
north star for your development. The second central idea is our role-based view. AI Literacy does not look the same for every function. For
example, a consultant, an application manager or a project manager do not need identical depth for all conceivable AI usages.

Thus, all roles need a common frame that enables collaboration across business, technology and support functions but different depths. We
use three levels per AI capability: Acquire, Deepen and Create. Acquire represents the foundational level, where people build a reliable
understanding of concepts, opportunities, limits and safe usage patterns. Deepen reflects the point at which teams apply AI with more role-
specific judgement, stronger methods and clearer integration into day-to-day work. Create marks the highest ambition level, where roles
actively redesign offerings, decisions, workflows or services through AI and contribute to scalable organizational value creation. To give you
an idea of “how it will feel” to work with AI in the future, we drafted a current state and a target state per role.

The actual measures to build up the AI capabilities are courses, hands-on sessions, blended learning sessions, etc. The Self-Assessment
outputs the corresponding courses for your specific context, and on the pages of the LHIND Academy you will find the courses and blended
learning sessions. The AI Literacy team is continuously evaluating new courses and learning formats and platforms and will ma ke them
available to you. Enjoy the journey. Have fun. We look forward to your feedback.
