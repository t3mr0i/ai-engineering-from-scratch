One-sentence: Hairline-divided accordion for FAQ-style content; caret rotates, smooth height.

```jsx
<Accordion items={[
  {title:'What is the baggage allowance?', content:'One cabin bag up to 8 kg…'},
  {title:'Can I change my flight?', content:'Yes, fare conditions apply…'},
]} defaultOpen={[0]} />
```
`multi` keeps several panels open at once.
