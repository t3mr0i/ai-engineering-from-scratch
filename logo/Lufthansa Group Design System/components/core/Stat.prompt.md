One-sentence: The Lufthansa Group hero statistic — a big thin number, optionally wrapped in a fine progress arc (the annual-report look).

```jsx
<Stat value="1.673" unit="Mio €" label="Result" tone="core" />
<Stat value="85" progress={85} label="Growth in %" tone="purple" />
```

`progress` (0–100) switches to the ring presentation. Tones map to brand + Expressions colours. Keep numbers factual and metric-first.
