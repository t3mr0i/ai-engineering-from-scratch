One-sentence: Pill badge for status and category labels; sentence case, subtle tints.

```jsx
<Badge tone="blue">On time</Badge>
<Badge tone="success" icon="ph-check">Confirmed</Badge>
<Badge tone="teal">Sustainable</Badge>
```

Tones: `neutral | blue | success | warning | error | teal | purple`. Accent tones (teal/purple) map to the Expressions palette — use for category coding, not chrome.
