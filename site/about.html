About the LHIND AI Learning Catalog

A working curriculum for technology consultants who build and ship AI systems for clients. Each lesson reads primary sources, ships a runnable artefact, and ends on something a TC keeps: a prompt, a rubric, an agent, or an MCP server.

Why it exists

You can finish an AI playlist and still not be able to explain a loss curve, or wire a tool into an agent without knowing what attention is doing inside the model that calls it. This curriculum is built around that gap. Every task starts from the underlying math or spec, runs against the production library, and ends on something a TC can hand a steering committee, an InfoSec reviewer, or a procurement officer.

How the lessons are made

Drafts are produced with AI assistance and reviewed against primary sources. Where a lesson states a fact, it cites the original — an RFC, a spec, a research paper — not a secondary summary. Corrections and lesson ideas are tracked in the open on GitLab and land in the next revision.

The catalog, search index, sitemap, and llms.txt are regenerated on every deploy from the lesson Markdown in the repository, so the published numbers cannot drift from the source. Hosting is run internally at LHIND; the source is the canonical artefact.

Who builds it

Maintained by Lufthansa Industry Solutions (LHIND) engineers and the LHIND technology-consulting community. The source is MIT-licensed.
